Kraken Brewing is a Brisbane-based homebrew and craft beer blog. This site is a collection of recipes, notes and experiments from my time homebrewing beer.

I started in 2019 with a [mead](/post/2019-05-16-mead/) and a [brew-in-a-bag Pacific Ale](/post/2019-05-16-snw-clone-biab/) on the kitchen stove. These days I brew all-grain on a [Brewzilla](/post/2019-11-10-new-setup/), pressure ferment in a Fermzilla inside a temperature-controlled fridge, and keg using closed transfers.

## What you'll find here

- **Recipes and brew days**: full grain bills, hop schedules, water chemistry and how each beer turned out, good or bad.
- **Hazy IPAs and NEIPAs**, and a long-running fight against oxidation. If your hoppy beers are going dull, start with my [tips to avoid oxidation](/post/2023-12-27-tips-to-avoid-oxidation-in-homebrewed-beer/).
- **Fruited sours**, from kettle sours to Philly Sour yeast, with strawberry, mango, passionfruit, berries and more.
- **Clones of Aussie craft beers**, including Stone & Wood, Balter, Ballistic and Bracket Brewing, plus recipes from Hoppy Days Brewing Supplies.

Browse the [tags](/tags/) to find a style, or follow along on Instagram at [@kraken.brewing](https://www.instagram.com/kraken.brewing/).
